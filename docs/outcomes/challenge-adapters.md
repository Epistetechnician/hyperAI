# Submission-source adapters

The HSAI-owned local submission ledger provides cursor-based append-only
observation, exact duplicate handling, immutable source revisions, source and
receipt times, provenance checks, finalized cutoffs, and explicit completeness.

zk.golf and ecdsa.fail are represented by dated recorded-fixture profiles until
official stable live interfaces, terms, authoritative timestamps, pagination,
and completeness models are verified. 247CTF is manual reviewed metadata only.
All three profiles disable live reads, authenticated automation, and completeness
attestation and publish exact blockers in `config/source-profiles.json`.

An archive or manual fixture cannot establish that every eligible live
submission was observed. It cannot support a negative financial resolution.
