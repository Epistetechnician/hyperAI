# Federation Charter

## Membership

The public layer is open to read and submit. Sensitive evidence, review, and incident coordination require verified roles, least privilege, conflict disclosures, and an auditable access record.

Syndicates may represent research and evaluation, workers and whistleblowers, affected communities, legal and policy work, infrastructure and security, or communications and education. No single organization controls publication or signing.

## Release authority

Each syndicate signs its own content-addressed bundle. Federation validators verify schemas, digests, signatures, status transitions, and disclosure requirements. A mirror may reject a bundle but may not rewrite it.

## Appeals and conflicts

Affected parties receive a response window where feasible. Challenges create append-only dispute records. Reviewers recuse themselves from conflicts. The independent review board is separate from operational publication staff.

## Emergency boundary

Two-key authorization is limited to predeclared, reversible defensive actions in explicitly authorized environments. Every action has scope, TTL, kill switch, rollback, and audit logging. There is no emergency authority for third-party access, disruption, retaliation, or exploit release.
