# JustAI Charter

## Purpose

JustAI is a public-interest observatory and federation for making frontier AI risks, incidents, commitments, and accountability processes more visible, verifiable, and democratically governable.

## Non-negotiable limits

JustAI does not perform unauthorized access, disruption, sabotage, doxxing, harassment, coercion, exploit publication, market manipulation, uncontrolled autonomous activity, or general capability scaling. Defensive evaluation requires written authorization, an allowlist, a sandbox, fixed budgets, independent review, a kill switch, rollback, and an auditable expiry.

## Principles

Human rights, dignity, democratic control, nonviolence, privacy, due process, independence, contestability, data minimization, and scientific honesty. A claim must never exceed its evidence. `Unknown`, `NoCandidate`, `Contested`, and `Withdrawn` are first-class outcomes.

## Governance

The public observatory is separated from membership advocacy. A seven-member elected council, independent review board, privacy and safety steward, conflict disclosures, and affected-party response processes govern publication. Sensitive evidence is role-gated and never placed in public releases.
