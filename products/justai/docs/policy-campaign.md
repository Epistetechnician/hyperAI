# Pacing and Incident Accountability Compact

The first policy campaign converts verified public evidence into specific, lawful demands. It does not endorse candidates or parties and does not authorize harassment or disruption.

## Demands

1. Standardized incident and near-miss reporting compatible with the OECD, PAI, and other established reporting systems.
2. Independent predeployment and post-incident review for high-risk frontier systems.
3. Publicly useful incident summaries with protected technical reports for regulators and affected parties.
4. Worker anti-retaliation channels and independent whistleblower support.
5. Measurable pacing or pause triggers for high-risk capability development and automated AI research.
6. Affected-community representation and rights-impact assessment.

## Accountability record

Every campaign item links a source digest, evidence record, bounded claim, demand, target institution, lawful channel, response deadline, response, and outcome. Unsupported assertions remain `Submitted`, `Contested`, or `Unknown`.

## Organizational separation

Nonpartisan research publication and membership advocacy use separate governance, finances, personnel conflict records, and legal review. Entity and lobbying decisions require qualified counsel.
