# Legal and Privacy Boundary

This document is an engineering boundary, not legal advice. Counsel must review the entity structure, employment and whistleblower issues, privacy obligations, defamation risk, trade secrets, NDAs, computer misuse law, lobbying, tax status, and international participation before sensitive intake or advocacy begins.

The planned structure has two lanes: a nonpartisan research/observatory lane and a separate membership/advocacy lane. Their finances, publication authority, personnel conflicts, and records must remain separately auditable.

The protected-intake vault is not part of the public repository. It requires separate keys, least-privilege access, two-person approval for sensitive access, immutable access logging, retention and deletion rules, legal-hold handling, breach response, and an external security review.

No raw submission, secret, personal-data collection, or exploit detail belongs in Git, chat, build logs, public bundles, or generated site output.
